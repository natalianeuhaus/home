I AM JANE DOE

We are Jane Doe. An encounter
becomes an image.
An image becomes a wall.
01 / The installation

One person enters the frame.
Many faces fill the wall.

I AM JANE DOE is a participatory installation by Natalia Neuhaus. A red-lit room, a mirror, a camera, and a printer turn a private encounter into a public image. Each portrait is printed and added to a collective wall — a growing record of presence, visibility, and refusal to remain silent. The installation is part of a larger project confronting sexual, physical, and psychological violence and the struggle to be heard.
