Production, storage, and disposal

The radioactive slag could have come from any of these sites.

These sites produced, handled, stored, or received radioactive material. Later findings cannot always be traced to one source.

Liquid waste

Liquid radioactive waste was dumped into sewers, waterways, and disposal wells.

Records describe liquid and process wastes entering disposal wells, sewers, creeks, and rivers—pathways that could carry contamination beyond the place where it was produced.

November 4, 2017

New York required radiation screening—but not at every landfill.

The rule covers landfills that accept municipal solid waste or drilling and production waste. Landfills accepting neither waste stream are not required by this provision to use fixed radiation detectors.

The present-day gap

Reused fill may never pass through a radiation detector.

Covered landfills must screen incoming loads. But excavated material, slag, and reused fill can move directly to another property or project—and New York’s routine fill analysis does not list radionuclides.
